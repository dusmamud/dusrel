Ricsline is a free LRC maker: it turns plain song lyrics into a synced
`.lrc` file, where every line carries the timestamp at which it is sung.
Open the maker at [dusmamud.github.io/ricsline/](https://dusmamud.github.io/ricsline/)
and work through the steps below. No sign-up is needed, and your audio
never leaves your browser — the MP3 is processed locally on your device.

## Create a synced lyrics file

<img src="/docs-sections/how-to-create-synced-lyrics-lrc-with-ricsline-s1.jpg" alt="Create a synced lyrics file" width="700" height="394">


1. Open [Ricsline](https://dusmamud.github.io/ricsline/) and go to the
   maker.
2. Paste or type your lyrics, one line per row. You can also import an
   existing `.lrc` file if you want to fix or re-sync it — its tags and
   metadata are read in for you.
3. Add your MP3 file so you can listen while you sync.
4. Press play. Every time a new line starts in the song, tap the sync
   button (or press `Spacebar`). That stamps the current time onto the
   line.
5. When you reach the end of the song, every line has a timestamp in
   `[mm:ss.xx]` format — minutes, seconds, and hundredths of a second.
6. Export your work: download the `.lrc` file, copy the LRC text, or
   copy the JSON structure. You can also save it to the in-browser
   library to come back to later.

## Fix timing mistakes

<img src="/docs-sections/how-to-create-synced-lyrics-lrc-with-ricsline-s2.jpg" alt="Fix timing mistakes" width="700" height="394">


You do not need to start over if a line is off:

- **Click a line to seek.** Clicking any lyric line jumps the audio to
  that line's timestamp, so you can hear exactly where it lands.
- **Edit a timestamp inline.** If a line is slightly early or late, edit
  its timestamp directly instead of re-tapping the whole song.
- **Shift everything at once.** If the whole song is off by a constant
  amount — for example, because of a long intro — use the offset shifter
  to move all timestamps forward or back by a set number of seconds in
  one step.

## Tips

<img src="/docs-sections/how-to-create-synced-lyrics-lrc-with-ricsline-s3.jpg" alt="Tips" width="700" height="394">


- **Sync in short passes.** Do one full pass without worrying about
  perfection, then replay and fix the lines that feel off. It is faster
  than trying to get every tap perfect the first time.
- **Listen for line starts, not line ends.** Tap the moment you hear the
  first word of a line — that is the timestamp a lyrics player will use.
- **Keep your work in the library.** Saved files stay in your browser,
  so you can reopen and adjust them later without re-importing anything.
