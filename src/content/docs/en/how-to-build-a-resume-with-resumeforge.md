ResumeForge is a free resume builder that runs entirely in your browser.
You type your details, watch the resume take shape in a live A4 preview,
and export it as a PDF. No sign-up is needed, and your data never leaves
your device — drafts are autosaved in your browser.

Open it at [dusmamud.github.io/resumeforge/](https://dusmamud.github.io/resumeforge/).

## Build your resume

![Build your resume](/docs-sections/how-to-build-a-resume-with-resumeforge-s1.jpg)


1. Open [ResumeForge](https://dusmamud.github.io/resumeforge/).
2. Pick a template. There are four ATS-friendly templates — Minimal,
   Professional, Modern, and Classic — each with a live preview, so you
   can see the style before you commit to it.
3. Fill in your details: personal information, work experience,
   education, skills, and any other sections you need. The A4 preview
   updates as you type, so you always see exactly what the finished page
   will look like.
4. Adjust the look: change the accent colour, pick a font, and turn your
   photo on or off, depending on what the job and the template call for.
5. When you are happy with the preview, export it as a PDF via print and
   save or print the file.

Because drafts autosave in your browser, you can close the tab and come
back to your resume later without losing your work.

## Tips

![Tips](/docs-sections/how-to-build-a-resume-with-resumeforge-s2.jpg)


- **Write bullets as results, not duties.** Instead of listing what a
  job involved, say what you achieved — start with an action word and
  add a number where you can, for example how many people, orders, or
  projects were involved. Short, concrete bullets read better than long
  paragraphs.
- **Start with the content, then the style.** Write all your sections
  first in any template, then try the other templates — switching does
  not make you retype anything, and the preview shows each style
  instantly.
- **Keep it to the pages the preview shows.** If your resume spills onto
  an extra page, trim older roles or shorten bullet points rather than
  shrinking everything down.
- **Match the template to the role.** Minimal and Classic suit formal or
  traditional fields; Modern and Professional work well when you want a
  bit more visual structure.
- **Check the PDF before sending it.** Export once, open the PDF, and
  read it through — a quick check catches overflow, cut-off lines, and
  typos that are easy to miss in the editor.
- **Your drafts live in this browser.** Autosave uses your browser's own
  storage, so your resume is on this device only. Export the PDF as your
  portable copy if you switch devices.
